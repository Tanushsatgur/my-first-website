Our Story & Philosophy

Founded in early 2026, STRIDE emerged from a simple observation: the footwear industry was divided between ugly orthopedic shoes and stylish sneakers that caused long-term joint pain. We set out to bridge that gap. Our headquarters serves as a laboratory where we experiment with carbon-fiber plates, recycled ocean plastics, and breathable knit fabrics to create the future of movement.

Sustainability is at our core. We recognize that the fashion industry has a significant environmental footprint, which is why we've committed to a zero-waste manufacturing process. By 2027, our goal is for every single component of a STRIDE shoe—from the laces to the sole—to be 100% biodegradable or recyclable. We believe in leaving a legacy of health, not just for our customers, but for the planet.

Common Questions

How do I choose the correct size?

STRIDE shoes are designed to fit true to size based on international athletic standards. However, due to our integrated compression knit technology, the fit can feel snug at first. If you prefer a roomier feel or have a wider foot profile, we recommend choosing a half-size larger than your standard shoe size.

What is your return and exchange policy?

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. We offer a 30-day "Perfect Fit" guarantee. If your shoes don't feel exactly right, you can return them for a full refund or exchange, provided they are in their original packaging and haven't been worn outdoors. We even provide the return shipping label for free!

How should I clean my STRIDE shoes?

To maintain the integrity of the teal-pigmented fabrics, we recommend hand washing with a mild detergent and cool water. Avoid machine washing or high-heat drying, as this can degrade the specialized adhesive bonds in the midsole. Let them air dry in a shaded area for the best results.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, STRIDE currently ships to over 50 countries worldwide. Shipping times vary by location, but most international orders arrive within 7-12 business days. Please note that customs fees or import duties may apply depending on your country's regulations.
